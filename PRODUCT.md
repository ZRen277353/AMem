# Product

## Register

product

## Users

AMem serves technical users performing repeated Android memory-debugging and
reverse-engineering workflows on Windows. They need to connect to a device,
select a process, inspect large amounts of state, and perform potentially
destructive operations without losing track of the active target or outcome.

## Product Purpose

AMem provides one desktop workspace for remote Android process inspection,
memory scanning and editing, hardware breakpoints, symbols, disassembly, Lua
automation, and an approval-aware native AI Agent. Success means experts can
move quickly while connection, target, cancellation, and mutation states remain
explicit and auditable.

## Brand Personality

Technical, restrained, and dependable. The interface should feel like a
focused debugging instrument: dense where comparison matters, direct in its
language, and calm around dangerous operations.

## Anti-references

Do not turn operational screens into marketing layouts, oversized dashboards,
decorative card collections, or animation-heavy experiences. Avoid novelty
controls, hidden state, low-contrast decoration, and visual effects that make
addresses, results, errors, or approval boundaries harder to scan.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the active connection and target legible wherever an operation depends
   on them.
2. Prefer compact tables and familiar controls for repeated expert workflows.
3. Make destructive actions, uncertain completion, and audit failures explicit
   without overstating what was cancelled or confirmed.
4. Reuse the existing component and semantic-color vocabulary across windows.
5. Keep diagnostic detail available while preventing secrets and raw memory
   content from leaking into status surfaces.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast in the dark theme, preserve keyboard and standard
ImGui interaction behavior, and never rely on color alone for status. Text,
tables, and controls must remain usable under Windows DPI scaling without
overlap or truncation of critical state. Avoid decorative motion; any future
state motion must respect reduced-motion preferences.
